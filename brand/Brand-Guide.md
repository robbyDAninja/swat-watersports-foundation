Review draft. Proposed additions need Dave's approval before public use.

## Page 1 Logo System
### Brand statement

Behind-the-boat fun for families and groups, brought to you.

### Logo introduction

Use the detailed logo when there is room for the whole story. Use the simplified logo when clarity at a smaller size matters.

### Logo rules

- Keep the original artwork intact. Use one main detailed version consistently.
- Leave empty space of about one-tenth of the mark's width on every side.
- Start at 160 pixels wide for the detailed logo and 220 for the simple logo. Keep WATERSPORTS readable.
- Use the wave icon only where SWAT is identified nearby. It is not the full business name.

### File note

Original logos: 480 x 480 PNGs. Simplified marks: PNG design candidates. Larger print needs suitable production artwork; no vector or stitch files are included.

## Page 2 Shirts and Small Uses

### Shirt direction

For a collared boating shirt, use the simple logo in one thread color on the wearer's left chest. Navy suits a light shirt; white suits a navy shirt.

### Light label

Navy mark / light shirt

### Dark label

White-on-navy reference

### Shirt caption

Placement sketch only. Actual position depends on pockets, seams, and the shirt.

### Vendor checklist

- Start around 4 inches wide; let the vendor confirm the fit and readable letter size.
- Send the simple-logo PNG and request one-color embroidery. The navy file has a transparent background; the white reference has a navy background.
- Ask the vendor to make the machine stitch file and a sample on the actual shirt before a full order.

### Embroidery note

The artwork is not a machine stitch file. Tiny text, fine lines, and sunset shading are the reason to use the simpler mark.

### Other uses

Simple logo: small signs, invoices, and merchandise. Detailed logo: larger website features, flyers, and full-color displays.

## Page 3 Colors and Type

### Palette introduction

Four everyday colors, with clear jobs. The sunset's yellow and the lettering's silver stay in the illustration; they do not need to become website colors.

### Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Deep Navy | #102B3A | Headings, text, dark backgrounds |
| Primary | White | #FFFFFF | Open space and light backgrounds |
| Secondary | Water Blue | #249AED | Buttons, links, water details |
| Accent | Sunset Orange | #FE6F2B | A small highlight when needed |

### Color rules

- Let navy and white lead. Add blue for useful emphasis; keep orange occasional.
- Use navy text on white or blue, and white text on navy. Avoid white body text on bright blue or orange.
- Keep the supplied logo's original colors. Printed ink and thread are matched by the vendor.

### Typography

| Use | Font | Setting |
|---|---|---|
| Headings | Barlow Condensed | Bold 700; short, strong headings |
| Body and buttons | Inter | Regular 400; Semibold 600 for buttons |

### Heading example

FUN BEHIND THE BOAT.

### Body example

Bring family and friends together for a day on the water.

### Type rules

Website starting sizes: headings 40-56 pixels; body 16-18 pixels. Keep paragraphs short. Use Arial or sans-serif if the brand fonts are unavailable. Do not retype the logo.

## Page 4 Voice and Website Direction

### Tone

Friendly, energetic, and welcoming. Say what the experience is, who it is for, and how to ask about an outing.

### Proposed website copy

| Element | Copy |
|---|---|
| Headline | Fun behind the boat. Brought to you. |
| Description | Mobile watersports experiences for families, friends, and groups. |
| Contact button | Ask about a group outing |

### Message focus

| Focus | Guidance |
|---|---|
| The experience | Show the fun of activities behind the towing boat. |
| The people | Help families, friends, and groups picture a day together. |
| The convenience | Explain SWAT coming to the group, with locations confirmed by Dave. |

### Photography

Use Dave-approved pictures of the actual boat, activities, and people enjoying time together. Keep them natural and uncluttered.

### Writing rule

Publish confirmed activities, service areas, prices, and contact details. Avoid claims such as "safest" or "best" without support.

### Kit contents

Logo PNGs, Barlow Condensed and Inter font files with licenses, website color/type styles, and an editable guide for Erinn.

### Review before public use

Dave confirms the main logo, simplified marks, colors, fonts, copy, and business details.
