A few questions
before we go.

Have another one? Send us a text.

Can I try this if I’ve never done it?

Tell us what you’re interested in and your experience level. We’ll talk through the setup and which activities are suitable before arranging your outing.

What equipment is available?

Ask about the Easy Up skiing setup, wakeboarding, and tubing. Confirm the equipment and fit for each rider when you book.

Where do we meet?

It depends on your outing. Share your dock, ramp, boat location, or proposed pickup spot and we’ll confirm a workable meeting place.

What should we bring?

Swimwear, a towel, drinking water, and sun protection are a good start. Ask about any other requirements for your group when you arrange the day.

How do we book?

Call or text (561) 801-0080 with your preferred date, group size, location, and the rides you have in mind. We’ll discuss availability and the next steps.

What if the weather changes?

Ask about the weather and rescheduling policy when you book. Confirm the day’s arrangements before heading to your meeting place.
